# Code of Conduct

Nubli follows a simple standard: be respectful, constructive and privacy-conscious.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Do not share confidential documents or personal data.
- Help users avoid unsafe anonymization practices.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else’s private data.
- Uploading confidential documents to public issues.
- Pressuring maintainers to weaken safety checks without review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments/issues that expose sensitive data or violate this code.
